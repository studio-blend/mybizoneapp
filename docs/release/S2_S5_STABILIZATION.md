## Supported scope and accounting limits

- Returns require an original document, use its stored discounts/tax/value, enforce remaining quantity and stock, and restock all returned goods. Cash returns record a return document but **do not record cash disbursement**. Damaged/non-restock returns, refund payout and advance-credit accounting are unsupported.
- Credit returns are supported for unreconciled-payment-free parties with adequate recorded debt (customer bill must still be due). **Any recorded general settlement for the party blocks automatic credit returns**, even if that settlement may concern another bill. This conservative restriction is necessary until invoice allocation exists. Reconciliation is an external/manual prerequisite; this release adds no bypass or reconciliation UI. Imported/manual historical balances require verification before use.
- General settlements remain party-level; invoice due status and due-bill/balance-sheet reports must not be assumed to reconcile those receipts. Overpayments/advances and per-invoice allocation are unsupported. EMI schedules reserve principal from general receipts; only full scheduled instalments are accepted. EMI returns, restructuring, cancellation, partial instalments and lender-interest accounting are unsupported. EMI interest does not reduce customer principal.
- Duplicate product lines with identical cost snapshots still use weighted original monetary value; a specific original line cannot be selected. Differing cost snapshots are blocked. Missing cost snapshots remain missing, never invented.
- Closing quantity is reconstructed from live stock and recorded movements, with purchases using their entered purchase date. Backdated purchases can restate historical stock; this is not an immutable period-close ledger. Unrecorded legacy edits/direct database writes cannot be reconstructed. Historical valuation uses **current saved product cost**, not FIFO, weighted average or historical cost. Deleted/inactive products present in the database remain eligible; permanently removed source data cannot be recovered.
- P&L uses saved sale/return costs, includes free stock and excludes GST from sales/return revenue. Missing costs are excluded, so reported profit is incomplete/overstated when costs are absent. Damage/write-off cost, depreciation, inventory revaluation and full accrual/double-entry accounting are not included automatically. Purchases currently record tax as zero and do not provide an input-tax-credit ledger. Profit-by-item/brand is gross margin before returns and expenses; brand attribution uses current product metadata. No GST-return/e-invoice filing or statutory correctness certification was performed.
- Historical GST sales without trustworthy persisted invoice tax cannot be safely backfilled from current settings; first issuance is blocked pending reconciliation. Existing invoice PDFs are reused. Missing-PDF recovery can preserve only stored fields: business name/customer phone and rendering template are not fully frozen. PDF upload and database commit are not one storage transaction; a failed database commit after upload may leave an orphan file. Actual renderer/storage failure acceptance remains Phase 2.

No unresolved implementation/test blocker remains for this bounded S2/S5 scope. These unsupported cases and accounting limitations still require owner/accountant acceptance before customer launch.
